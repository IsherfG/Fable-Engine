## The three booklets

Each covers a different pillar, and each is self-contained so a different person can DM
each one:

1. **Wilderness** — exploration. Four regions; Region 1 is the Trail, Region 2 is the
   Woods. Two further regions are named only in the book.
2. **Keep on the Borderlands** — social. The party's only safe base.
3. **Caves of Chaos** — combat. A–K dungeon crawls.

**Recommended running order (official DM tips):** Wilderness → arrival at the Keep →
Caves of Chaos, then free movement between all three.

## Load-bearing mechanics

- **Long rests only work inside the Keep.** This is the spine of the whole campaign: it
  forces the back-and-forth loop between safety and the caves. Any rest elsewhere is at
  best a short rest.
- **Levels are gained by completing caves**, so the caves are effectively the XP track.
- **Factions have agendas and can be allied with.** The tribes are not just targets;
  players can build alliances, run subterfuge, or play factions against each other.
- **The Chaos Bell** is the Cult of Chaos's power source. Its nature and the method to
  destroy it are described in an **iron tome in C3 Library** (Cave C, Nothic Lair).

## Structure — Keep on the Borderlands

Only these areas are confirmed by public sources:

- **Outer gatehouse** — guards **Bartho** and **Gala** intercept arrivals.
- **Fortress** — **Winvarle**, the castellan, plus advisers. Briefs the party on the Caves.
- **Area 8 — traveler's inn** — **Haldryck** can be found here.
- **Area 9 — tavern** — meals and rumours.
- **Area 11 — temple** — its previous priest, **Ivlis**, left under mysterious circumstances.

Areas 1–7 and 10 are not published publicly. **Fill them in from your booklet.**

## Structure — Wilderness

- **Region 1 — Trail** (between keep and caves; works approached from the south)
- **Region 2 — Woods** (the more direct route between caves and keep)
- Two more regions, unnamed in public sources.

Confirmed trail encounters, in the order the official tips suggest running them:

1. **Looted Wagon** — establishes that bandits are active.
2. **Game Trail** — meet **Haldryck**, learn about the Keep.
3. **Bandit Ambush** — **Pral** and his bandits. If Pral wins he can become a recurring
   antagonist, which is a useful thread for a small party.

## Two players, one AI DM

The box targets 2–4 characters, so **two PCs is in range** — no house rules needed.
Practical adjustments for a duo:

- Two characters cover fewer roles. Expect missing healing or missing trap-handling;
  let the DM lean on allies (Haldryck, Oggdug, faction allies) rather than nerfing monsters.
- Combat written for four will overrun two. Prefer **fewer monsters** over weaker
  monsters, so the fiction stays intact.
- The Keep's NPCs and faction alliances become much more valuable — a small party needs
  friends. Push social play.
